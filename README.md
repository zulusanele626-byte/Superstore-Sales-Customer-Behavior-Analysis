# Sales & Business Performance Analytics Using SQL

To analyze sales, customer, and operational data using SQL in order to generate insights that support business performance and decision-making.

## Objectives 

To analyze sales performance across categories, regions, and time periods using SQL
To identify key revenue drivers and profitability trends within the business
To evaluate customer purchasing behavior and order frequency
To measure sales consistency and trends over time
To assess delivery efficiency across different shipping modes
To segment customers and understand their contribution to total sales
To transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights

## KPI Questions
What is the total sales and average order value by category?

Which customers place the most frequent orders?

How do sales trends vary over time (monthly and yearly)?

Which shipping mode has the best delivery efficiency?

Which customer segments contribute the most to revenue?

What are the overall sales patterns across products and regions?

Who are the most valuable customers based on total spending?

## Processes 
Data extraction from Superstore dataset

Data exploration and preview of raw records

Data cleaning (handling duplicates and null values where applicable)

Data aggregation using GROUP BY for analysis

Time-based analysis using YEAR and MONTH functions

Customer segmentation using COUNT and SUM functions

Delivery efficiency calculation using DATEDIFF function

Ranking and filtering of top customers and performance metrics

Generation of business insights using SQL queries

## Projects Insight
Certain product categories generate significantly higher revenue, showing strong concentration of sales performance

A small group of customers contributes a large portion of total sales, indicating high customer dependency

Sales performance shows clear monthly and yearly fluctuations, suggesting seasonal trends

Standard shipping mode is widely used but not always the most time-efficient

Customer segments differ significantly in their contribution to overall revenue

Top customers and repeat buyers play a key role in driving business profitability

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights. By analyzing sales, customers, and operational performance, key patterns and trends were identified that support strategic decision-making.

The analysis highlights the importance of customer segmentation, product performance tracking, and delivery efficiency in improving overall business performance. This project showcases strong SQL skills in data aggregation, filtering, and business-oriented analysis.
